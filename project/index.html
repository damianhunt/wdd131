PNC560 5G XSecure Rugged Device

  • Dual 3W ultra linear speakers bring louder sound effects 5G + 3 high-resolution cameras to ensure high definition video and high-speed streaming
  • Detachable high-capacity battery and optional charger cater to user preferences
